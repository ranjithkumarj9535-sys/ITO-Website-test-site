About Indian Talent Olympiad

Discover what we do and offer our students

ITO – Indian Talent Olympiad – is one of the world’s leading Olympiad organisations. Established in 2013, it is proudly associated with 42,000+ schools.

With a strong presence across India, ITO has reached 1 crore+ students, recognised 2 lakh+ class toppers and awarded 20,000+ scholarships. The organisation is honoured to have Olympian champion P.T. Usha on its advisory committee, a name synonymous with excellence and achievement.

We believe talent thrives when given the right opportunities, and we are committed to empowering millions of students to realise their true potential.

Read Our StoryExplore Olympiad Programs

Why Indian Talent Olympiad?

Empowering students nationwide to discover their talents, develop critical skills and achieve academic excellence through recognition and rewards – while building a strong foundation for JEE and NEET.

What is the Indian Talent Olympiad?

A leading platform that conducts innovative Olympiad exams, provides comprehensive study materials and offers scholarships, helping learners strengthen conceptual clarity and problem-solving skills.

How does ITO work?

Students register online or through their schools, take subject-wise Olympiad exams, and receive detailed performance feedback, rankings and scholarships.

What are Olympiad exams?

National-level tests that grow young minds

Olympiad exams are national-level competitive tests designed to nurture logical thinking, problem-solving and academic excellence from an early age. Why participate? To challenge yourself, gain recognition and grow beyond the school curriculum.

How it works

Three simple steps to the Olympiad

Whether you register through your school or on your own, the journey is the same.

Not sure which route is right for you? Read the frequently asked questions or call our toll-free helpline on 1800 266 9192.

  1. Register

    Schools enrol students (minimum 40) through the school registration form. Individual students sign up online.

  2. Prepare

    Practise with workbooks, previous year papers and online mock tests designed for your class.

  3. Compete & win

    Take the exam on your scheduled date, check your rank in the ITO app and collect medals, certificates and scholarships.

FAQs

Questions parents ask

What is the Indian Talent Olympiad?

Indian Talent Olympiad (ITO) is a national-level Olympiad organisation founded in 2013 by academicians and scientists. It conducts subject Olympiads for students from Nursery to Class 12 to identify talent, build confidence and reward excellence with medals, certificates and scholarships.

Which boards does the Olympiad syllabus follow?

Questions are based on the prescribed syllabus of CBSE, ICSE, State Boards and International Boards for each class, with additional logical reasoning and higher-order thinking (HOTS) questions.

How can my child register for the Olympiad?

Most students register through their school – the school enrols at least 40 students and submits one registration. Students in Classes 1–12 can also register individually for the online Olympiad. Nursery to Senior KG children take part through the Little Champ Olympiads.

What is the exam pattern?

For Classes 1–4 the paper has 35 questions and for Classes 5–10 it has 50 questions, each carrying 1 mark, across subject, logical reasoning and HOTS sections. The offline exam lasts 65 minutes and the online exam 45 minutes (Drawing 60, Essay 40).

What awards and scholarships can students win?

National Rank 1 holders receive a cash scholarship of ₹1,00,000, with further cash awards for Ranks 2 to 4, plus medals, certificates and merit awards at state and zonal level.
